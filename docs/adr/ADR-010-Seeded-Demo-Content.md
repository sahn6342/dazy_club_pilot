# ADR-010: Seeded Demo Content

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Final media, copy, testimonials, and CMS workflows may not be available before design and first implementation.

## Decision
Use clearly replaceable seeded demo content for sports, gallery, testimonials, notifications, and public page content.

## Alternatives Considered
- Wait for final content before design.
- Build empty states only.

## Consequences
- Design and implementation can proceed.
- Seed content must be easy to replace.
- Demo content must not masquerade as final legal or factual copy.
