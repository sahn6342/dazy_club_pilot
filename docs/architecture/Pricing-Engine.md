# Pricing Engine

## Status
Deferred from first launch.

## Responsibilities
- Compute prices by sport, court, date, time, event, and override rules.
- Support future membership pricing.
- Provide price breakdowns to booking checkout.

## Rules
- Dynamic pricing is supported.
- Overrides may apply by sport, date, slot, or event.
- Pricing must be testable as pure business logic.

## Launch Boundary
Launch content may describe experiences but should not claim live dynamic pricing unless implemented.
