# Design Principles

## Premium First
Use dark surfaces, gold accents, strong photography zones, and restrained visual effects.

## Mobile First
Design mobile layouts before desktop expansion. CTAs and forms must be easy to use with thumbs.

## High Contrast
Text, icons, and controls must remain readable on dark backgrounds.

## Purposeful Motion
Motion should guide attention and reinforce polish, not distract from enquiry conversion.

## Honest Scope
Do not design launch screens as if booking, OTP, or payment are live.
