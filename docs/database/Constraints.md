# Constraints

## Launch
- Sports must have unique slugs.
- Venue slugs must be unique.
- Lead must include name and at least one contact method.
- CorporateEnquiry must include contact name and at least one contact method.
- GalleryItem status must be draft, published, or archived.
- Testimonial status must be draft, published, or archived.
- Notification status must be draft, active, or archived.

## Future
- Booking end time must be after start time.
- Booking duration must be at least 30 minutes.
- Booking interval must align to 15-minute blocks.
- Booking status must be constrained to approved states.
- Payment status must be constrained to approved states.
- OTP expiry must be after creation.
