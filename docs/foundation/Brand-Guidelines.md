# Brand Guidelines

## Personality
Premium, energetic, modern, and playful.

## Visual Direction
- Dark-first.
- Near-black backgrounds.
- Dark-gray surfaces.
- Gold primary accent.
- High contrast.
- Clean layouts with strong sports imagery.

## Experience Principles
- Make Dazy.club feel aspirational but usable.
- Lead with the sports experience, not internal systems.
- Keep booking language future-safe until live booking ships.
- Use confident CTAs for enquiry and corporate events.

## Content Tone
Clear, warm, energetic, and direct. Avoid overpromising live booking, instant confirmation, or payment if those features are not active.
