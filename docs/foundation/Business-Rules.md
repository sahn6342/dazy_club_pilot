# Business Rules

## Booking
- BR-001: Minimum booking duration = 30 minutes.
- BR-002: Booking interval = 15 minutes internally.
- BR-003: Multiple courts can be booked in one checkout.
- BR-004: Guest booking via OTP.
- BR-005: Payment modes: Full, Partial, Pay at Venue.

## Pricing
- BR-101: Dynamic pricing supported.
- BR-102: Override by sport, date, slot or event.
- BR-103: Future membership pricing supported.

## Sports
- Cricket turf can be reconfigured into 3 badminton courts.

## Notifications
- Full screen campaign.
- Modal campaign.
- Sticky banner.
- Toast notifications.
- Floating CTA.

## Gallery
- Sport-wise galleries.
- Albums by event.
- Admin approval required.
