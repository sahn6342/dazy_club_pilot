# Business Entities

## Launch Entities
- Venue: Dazy.club location and operating context.
- Sport: Cricket, Badminton, Pickleball.
- Court: Physical playable area.
- CourtConfiguration: Mapping between physical court layout and sports.
- Lead: General visitor enquiry.
- CorporateEnquiry: Corporate/event enquiry.
- GalleryItem: Public image/video proof item.
- Testimonial: Public social proof item.
- Notification: Public campaign/banner/message.

## Future Entities
- Customer: Verified visitor identity.
- Booking: Booking header.
- BookingItem: Sport/court/time allocation.
- PricingRule: Dynamic pricing rule.
- Payment: Payment attempt/status.
- OtpChallenge: OTP verification attempt.
- AuditLog: Admin/system activity history.
