# Acceptance Criteria

## Launch
- AC-001: Home communicates Dazy.club positioning in the first viewport.
- AC-002: Visitor can browse Cricket, Badminton, and Pickleball.
- AC-003: Visitor can submit a general enquiry.
- AC-004: Corporate organizer can submit a corporate/event enquiry.
- AC-005: Gallery and testimonials render from seeded or API-backed content.
- AC-006: Public screens are responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- AC-007: Form validation explains missing or invalid fields.
- AC-008: Enquiry submission success and failure states are visible.
- AC-009: No launch screen completes a fake live booking.
- AC-010: Payment and OTP are not required for launch.

## Future Booking
- AC-101: Guest completes booking in under 60 seconds.
- AC-102: Booking confirmation is shown immediately after successful payment or approved payment mode.
- AC-103: Availability updates without page refresh.
- AC-104: Payment failures recover gracefully.
