# User Personas

## P1 College Student
Wants a quick, premium place to play with friends. Needs fast sport discovery, pricing/availability hints when future booking launches, and an easy contact path.

## P2 Coach
Evaluates facility quality, court setup, and repeat usage potential. Needs clear sport detail pages, gallery proof, and direct enquiry.

## P3 Family
Looks for safe, well-maintained recreation. Needs trust signals, photos, testimonials, and simple contact options.

## P4 Corporate Organizer
Plans team events. Needs group capacity, event support, corporate enquiry form, and follow-up assurance.

## P5 Sports Enthusiast
Compares sports, surfaces, timings, and experience quality. Needs sport detail and future booking readiness.
