# Lead Capture

## Launch Lead Types
- General enquiry.
- Corporate/event enquiry.

## General Enquiry Fields
- Name.
- Phone or email.
- Interested sport.
- Message.

## Corporate Enquiry Fields
- Contact name.
- Company.
- Work email or phone.
- Event type.
- Estimated group size.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred sport or mixed sports.
- Message.

## Validation
- Name is required.
- At least one contact method is required.
- Corporate group size must be positive if provided.
- Message length should be bounded.

## Future CRM
Leads should be structured so CRM automation can later add statuses, owners, notes, follow-up reminders, and source attribution.
