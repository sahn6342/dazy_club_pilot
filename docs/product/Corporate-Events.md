# Corporate Events

## Goal
Let organizations enquire about team outings, tournaments, sports days, and private group experiences.

## Launch Content
- Corporate hero section.
- Event format examples.
- Sports supported: Cricket, Badminton, Pickleball.
- Group-size prompt.
- Preferred date/time prompt.
- Contact details.
- Message field.

## Form Fields
- Contact name.
- Company.
- Work email or phone.
- Event type.
- Estimated group size.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred sport or mixed sports.
- Message.

## Success State
Show a clear confirmation and explain that the team will follow up manually.
