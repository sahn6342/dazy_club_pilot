# Product Requirements Document

## Objective
Build Dazy.club as a premium sports experience platform. The first release is a polished public website that builds trust, explains the sports experience, and captures enquiries before live booking, OTP, and payment are enabled.

## Audience
- College students and friend groups looking for quick sports sessions.
- Families looking for safe recreation.
- Coaches and sports enthusiasts comparing facilities.
- Corporate/event organizers planning group sports events.

## Launch Scope
- Public website for browse and enquiry.
- Sports: Cricket, Badminton, Pickleball.
- Seeded content for sports, gallery, testimonials, notifications, and venue highlights.
- General contact enquiry.
- Corporate/event enquiry.
- Design-first Figma handoff before code.

## Deferred Scope
- Live availability and booking.
- OTP authentication.
- Payment collection.
- Full admin CMS, CRM automation, and notification campaign management.
- Membership, wallet, cafe, and mobile apps.

## Success Metrics
- Visitors understand the offer within the first viewport.
- Contact and corporate enquiry forms are easy to find and submit.
- Lighthouse score target greater than 95.
- Accessibility AA target.
- Mobile-first usability.
- No launch flow depends on payment, OTP, or live booking.

## Non-Negotiables
- Do not present a fake live booking confirmation in launch scope.
- Do not hardcode an OTP or payment provider.
- Preserve future booking/payment/OTP contracts behind adapter boundaries.
